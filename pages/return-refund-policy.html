Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 June 2026

At Cochin Wood Industries Private Limited, we want you to be satisfied with your purchase. This policy explains when and how you can return an item and receive a refund.

1. Return eligibility

  • You may request a return within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused, in their original condition and packaging, and accompanied by the invoice or order number.
  • Returns are accepted for items that arrive damaged, defective, or materially different from the description on our website.
  • Due to the nature of wood and timber products, items that have been cut, treated, polished, painted or customised to order are not eligible for return unless they are defective.

2. How to request a return

  1. Email info@cochinwood.in or call +91 95674 10175 within 7 days of delivery.
  2. Include your order number, the item(s) concerned, and photographs if the product is damaged or defective.
  3. Our team will confirm the return and share return-shipping instructions within 1–2 business days.

3. Refunds

  • Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will notify you of the approval of your refund.
  • Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method within 7–10 business days.
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable except where the return is due to our error (damaged, defective or wrong item).

4. Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled before dispatch for a full refund. Once an order has been dispatched, it can no longer be cancelled but may be returned under the conditions above.
