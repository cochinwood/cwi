Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 21 June 2026

This policy explains how Cochin Wood Industries Private Limited processes and delivers orders placed on www.cochinwood.in.

1. Order processing

  • Orders are processed and dispatched within 2–5 business days of payment confirmation.
  • Custom-cut or made-to-order items may require additional processing time, which will be communicated at the time of order.

2. Delivery time & coverage

  • We ship across India. Standard delivery typically takes 5–10 business days after dispatch, depending on your location and the size of the item.
  • Large or heavy timber items may be delivered via surface freight; estimated timelines are shown at checkout or confirmed by our team.

3. Shipping charges

  • Shipping charges, where applicable, are calculated and displayed at checkout before you pay. The price you see at checkout is the final price.
  • Any free-shipping thresholds, if offered, are shown on the relevant product or cart page.

4. Tracking & delays

Once your order ships, we will share tracking details (where available) by email or phone. If a delivery is delayed beyond the estimated window, please contact us and we will assist.
