About Cochin Wood Industries

Cochin Wood Industries Private Limited is a registered Indian company based in Kurupampadi, Ernakulam, Kerala, specialising in quality wood, plywood and timber products. We supply to homeowners, builders, carpenters and businesses across India, combining decades of regional woodworking expertise with reliable, transparent online ordering.

Our goal is simple: deliver genuine, well-sourced wood products at fair prices, backed by honest customer service. Every product we list is described accurately, priced clearly, and dispatched from our facility in Kerala.
